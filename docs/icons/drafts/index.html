Fan & Lighting icon drafts - preview only

Six clean-room 24dp vectors, three per family. Geometry follows the LineageOS 24 Settings language (960 viewport, 80-unit stroke = 2dp, rounded joins, one colour). Nothing here is wired into the app.

Drafts only. The vectors are not copied into AshFallParts/res and no app, tile or Settings code was changed. Pick a variant, then it can be promoted in a follow-up.
